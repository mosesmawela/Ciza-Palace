# CIZA (South African Afro-pop & Amapiano Artist) — Product Facts

## Overview
CIZA (born Ntsika Ciza) is a rising South African Afro-pop, African electronic, and Amapiano recording artist. He blends native South African Amapiano and Afro-beat rhythms with smooth, luxurious global electronic solfeggio layers to sculpt a distinct sonic signature.

## Key Biography & Lineage Facts
- **Full Name**: Ntsika Ciza
- **Hometown**: Johannesburg, South Africa
- **Duality of Lineage**: He is the son of South African music royalty:
  - **Mother**: Nhlanhla Mafu (celebrated lead singer of the legendary multi-award-winning Afro-pop group Mafikizolo)
  - **Father**: TK Nciza (prominent South African record label executive, co-founder of legendary TS Records, and politician)
- **Partnerships**: Strongly collaborated & co-published in association with **LVRN (Love Renaissance)**, Atlanta's premier creative collective and record label, alongside Warner Music Africa.

## Core Discography & Hit Tracks
- **Major Debut EP**: *Golden Boy Pack* (released in 2021)
